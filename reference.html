FAQ

Questions creators usually ask.

Do you only edit Minecraft videos?

Yes. My focus is Minecraft content, including YouTube videos, Shorts, and Twitch clips. That focus helps me understand the pacing, humor, gameplay context, and viewer expectations better.

What kind of creators are the best fit?

YouTubers and Twitch streamers who create gameplay, tutorials, farms, survival, hardcore, fun facts, challenges, or talking-head Minecraft content — especially creators who want to record more and edit less.

How fast can you deliver?

It depends on complexity, but the average is around 2 days for Shorts and around 5 days for long-form videos. Tight deadlines can be discussed before the project starts.

Do you do revisions?

Yes. Revisions are unlimited with the goal of making sure you are satisfied. The only note is that feedback should be clear and reasonable so we can protect the deadline.

How do you deliver the final files?

Delivery can happen however you prefer. If you do not have a preference, I deliver through a shared Google Drive folder.

Can you edit Twitch streams into Shorts?

Yes. I can take a stream moment and turn it into a cleaner vertical short, adding context when needed so the clip makes sense even to viewers who did not watch the full stream.

Can you handle 4K footage?

Yes. I can edit and export up to 4K, depending on the project requirements and the files provided.

What do you need to quote a project?

Send the type of video, raw footage length, target final length, deadline, reference style, and any must-have moments. I can then confirm the rate and turnaround.
